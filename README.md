I'm the kind of engineer who gets excited about **removing friction** — not adding features for the sake of it. I build convenience systems, automate the painful parts, and fine-tune models until they actually solve the problem. At my startup, I wear a lot of hats: **LLM fine-tuning on A100s**, **infra & security**, **RAG pipelines**, and the occasional **"why is this shell script eating all our RAM?"** alert at 2 AM via ntfy.

**Right now I'm obsessed with:** Robotics & Arduino projects 🦾

**Off the clock:** I'm a certified car nerd — I can talk about design, engineering, and long drives until the heat death of the universe. 🏎️
